## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the philosophy of **"Quiet Care" (안온한 안부)**. Designed for adult children keeping a warm, respectful, and non-intrusive watch over aging parents who live independently, the interface deliberately rejects the sterile, alarming language of medical telemetry and invasive surveillance. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Stance
- **Dignified & Privacy-First:** No invasive camera streams, no clinical biometrics, no patronizing status indicators. Care is communicated through ambient patterns of daily life (e.g., morning routine detected, tea kettle used, gentle evening rest).
- **Quiet Reassurance:** The core message is always grounded in peace of mind—"Nothing out of the ordinary today." The visual cadence calms anxiety rather than manufacturing urgency.
- **Warm Modern Consumer Tech:** Balances the sleek precision of contemporary software with organic warmth, soft atmospheric lighting, and human microcopy.

### Design Aesthetic
The aesthetic blends **Modern Warm Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- Pristine, breathing surfaces with airy whitespace.
- Soft, pillowed cards floating above a cool-morning canvas.
- Rounded contours and gentle chromatic transitions that echo morning sunlight through a clean window.

## Colors

The color system is formulated to maintain psychological comfort, avoiding the high-anxiety alarms of clinical environments. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FBFF`):** An ethereal, ultra-light cool white tinted with blue sky, representing morning light and open air.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Unblemished pure white, elevated above the tinted canvas to provide clear structure.
- **Primary Accent — Gentle Sage & Mint (`#059669` / `#10B981` / Tint `#E6F7F0`):** The primary signal of reassurance. Communicates healthy daily momentum, peaceful routines, and stability.
- **Secondary Accent — Serene Sky & Teal (`#0284C7` / `#0EA5E9` / Tint `#E0F2FE`):** Used for connectivity indicators, daily timelines, device health, and family communication flows.
- **Warm Amber Highlight (`#F59E0B` / Tint `#FEF3C7`):** Replaces alarming red notifications. Used for subtle deviations requiring a gentle nudge (e.g., "Mother is waking up a bit later than usual today").
- **Neutral Hierarchy:**
  - **Deep Slate Navy (`#1E293B`):** Anchors high-priority titles, reassuring status headers, and active states.
  - **Muted Slate (`#64748B`):** Secondary timestamps, ambient notes, and contextual hints.
  - **Border & Hairline (`#E8EFF7`):** Gentle separation strokes that prevent visual clutter.

## Typography

The typography couples geometric balance with humanist warmth. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides rounded terminals, open counters, and high legibility across small mobile displays, harmonizing seamlessly with modern Korean typefaces (such as Pretendard and SUIT).

### Principles
- **Clarity and Warmth:** Text weights remain clean and legible, avoiding ultra-thin hairpins or overly aggressive extra-bold cuts.
- **Empathetic Microcopy:** Headlines convey whole sentences that reassure, such as *"어머니의 아침이 평소처럼 평온하게 시작되었어요"* (Mother's morning has begun peacefully as usual).
- **High-Readability Numbers:** Numeric times and activity counters are styled with generous letter-spacing and medium-to-bold weights for effortless reading at a glance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a gentle, fluid single-to-multi column model, prioritizing calm focus on the parent's daily status card before revealing historical trends.

### Form Factors & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column stacked stream. Screen margins are locked at `1rem` (16px) with `space-md` gaps between modules to maximize surface touchability and breathing room.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 2-column asymmetric layout with `margin-tablet` (`2rem`). The primary live status card spans full width or sits adjacent to the daily timeline.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Centered max-width container (`1140px`) with multi-card distribution for multi-parent views or longitudinal activity summaries.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Generous internal padding within cards (`space-lg` / 24px) creates an unhurried, comfortable layout.
- Vertical space separates unrelated domains (e.g., Living Room Motion vs. Pill Dispenser) to prevent visual density from creating false urgency.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **Ambient Shadows** and **Tonal Outlines** rather than harsh, deep drops. The physical metaphor is soft Japanese paper or satin ceramic resting atop morning fog.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Level 1 (Ambient Card):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05)`
  Provides a faint, atmospheric lift tinted slightly with ambient sky blue and slate.
- **Level 2 (Active / Floating Pill):** `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`
  Used for active reassurance badges and primary call-to-action buttons.
- **Ghost Borders:** Every floating surface uses a crisp `1px` border in `#E8EFF7` to maintain definition without contrast friction.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature with deliberate pill shapes for status pills, action badges, and interactive chips.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Cards & Enclosures:** Curvature scales from `1rem` (16px) on compact widgets to `1.5rem` (24px) on primary hero reassurance containers (`rounded-xl`).
- **Touch Targets & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to evoke organic touchability, soft star-motif friendly contours, and friendliness.
- **Icon Enclosures:** Soft squircle frames (40% radius) tinted with 10% opacity tints of the status color.

## Components

### Hero Reassurance Card ("별일없어요" State)
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5rem` corner radius, `1px` border `#E8EFF7`, and Level 1 ambient shadow.
- **Indicator:** Large soft star or rounded blossom emblem with an organic gradient (`#10B981` to `#0EA5E9`).
- **Copy Hierarchy:** Headline-lg text affirming peace ("오늘도 평온한 하루를 보내고 계세요"), accompanied by a secondary timestamp ("마지막 움직임: 오전 10:24 주방").

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "안부 전화 걸기"):** Gradient fill from `#059669` to `#0284C7`, text pure white, full pill shape (`9999px`), `0.75rem` vertical and `1.5rem` horizontal padding.
- **Secondary / Soft Action:** Surface background `#F0FDF4`, label `#059669`, with zero border and smooth scale transition on tap.
- **Ghost Action:** Border `1px solid #E8EFF7`, background `#FFFFFF`, label `#64748B`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Normal Routine:** Background `#E6F7F0`, text `#059669`, soft dot indicator in `#10B981`.
- **Gentle Notice:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#D97706`, label "확인이 필요해요".
- **Family Connected:** Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#0284C7`.

### Timeline & Activity Feed
- **Visual Style:** Connected timeline with continuous soft vertical line (`#E2E8F0`, 2px wide).
- **Nodes:** Soft rounded circles containing micro-icons representing routine milestones (e.g., wake-up, medicine cabinet opened, living room light switched on).
- **Time Indicators:** Label-sm in `#94A3B8`, tabular numerals.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8EFF7`, corner radius `0.75rem`, text `#1E293B`, focus ring in `#0EA5E9` with 20% opacity blur.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Soft rounded boxes, filled with `#059669` checkmark upon selection; unselected states remain `#E2E8F0` hairline strokes.
